Moderate Interaction

Potential interaction detected between Amoxicillin and Ibuprofen.

Clinical Description

Concurrent use of NSAIDs (like Ibuprofen) and Penicillins (like Amoxicillin) can sometimes alter the excretion rate of the antibiotic, potentially leading to increased blood levels of the penicillin. While often not clinically significant in healthy individuals, it warrants attention.

Advice

May increase risk of stomach irritation. Take both medications with food. Monitor for unusual gastrointestinal discomfort. Consult your pharmacist if you have impaired renal function.
